# CONTROL 00 — NBEF Root Control

**Control:** 00  
**Title:** NBEF Root Control  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Home:** This document  
**Form:** Markdown (`.md`)  
**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Authorized by:** NBEF-CWC #5, from NBEF-CWC #1 through #4 and `Audit/NBEF-CONTROL-00-Drafting-Charter.md`

This document is the **NBEF Root Control**.

It is Root-lock text. It is not a complete education architecture. It is not a subordinate CONTROL. It does not implement software, education operations, or any non-NBEF framework.

---

## 1. Framework identity

**NBEF** means **Node-Based Education Framework**.

NBEF is an independent framework.

NBEF is the education framework/domain governed by the NBEF control-document hierarchy in this repository.

---

## 2. Independence

NBEF SHALL remain a separate authoritative domain framework.

NBEF SHALL NOT be treated as:

- a subdivision of NBBF;
- a renamed NBBF;
- an implementation of UNBKE;
- an implementation of CDT;
- an implementation of Runtime Republic;
- a collection of BlueprintLiberty essays; or
- a conventional school-reform program.

NBBF and NBEF are separate frameworks.

For NBEF architectural purposes, Runtime Republic represents a broader governmental operating concept within the Human Engineer’s overall work. It is not NBEF.

CDT, UNBKE, software implementations, Digital Twins, and Runtime Republic concepts SHALL NOT silently become NBEF doctrine.

BlueprintLiberty material MAY be used as informative source material and evidence of Human Engineer intent. BlueprintLiberty publication material is not automatically NBEF controlling authority. Any doctrine derived from such material requires explicit adoption into the NBEF control-document hierarchy.

Independence from NBBF, BlueprintLiberty, Runtime Republic, CDT, UNBKE, and other non-NBEF sources is a Root lock. Similarity is not authority. Useful precedent is not authority. Architectural elegance is not authority.

---

## 3. Purpose of this Root Control

CONTROL 00 is the constitutional/architectural root of NBEF.

Its purpose is limited to Root-lock subjects:

- framework identity;
- purpose of this Root Control;
- scope of this Root Control versus later CONTROLs;
- root authority and Human Engineer authority;
- authority hierarchy;
- subordinate-control relationship;
- independence from external frameworks;
- terminology governance at Root level;
- fail-closed behavior;
- prohibition against unauthorized invention;
- provenance;
- amendment and replacement governance;
- anti-drift safeguards; and
- boundaries of subordinate CONTROLs.

CONTROL 00 SHALL NOT attempt to define the complete education architecture.

Detailed doctrine SHALL belong in later CONTROLs under one-authoritative-home discipline.

---

## 4. Scope of this Root Control

This CONTROL is the home of Root identity, Root authority, hierarchy, independence, Root-level terminology governance, fail-closed Root rules, provenance and amendment Root rules, and subordinate-control boundaries.

This CONTROL SHALL NOT be the home of detailed education doctrine that belongs in a later CONTROL.

This repository records NBEF control authority in documents. It does not implement software, Digital Twin systems, Runtime Republic systems, APIs, or education operations.

Canonical NBEF CONTROL documents SHALL be written in Markdown (`.md`).

NBEF control documents SHALL be versioned with Git.

Work in this repository SHALL proceed by Controlled Work Cycle (CWC). A CWC may create only what it is authorized to create. Later CWCs SHALL NOT be auto-started.

---

## 5. Root authority

The Human Engineer is the sole authority to:

- create, adopt, amend, or withdraw NBEF CONTROL documents;
- designate source material as canonical, referenced, non-canonical, or out of scope;
- resolve unresolved authority; and
- authorize GitHub remotes, visibility, ownership, and push.

No agent holds Human Engineer authority.

No agent may invent NBEF architecture, silently create CONTROL authority, or treat a gap in the record as permission to fill it.

Repository-bound agents, including any steward bound to this repository, SHALL fail closed on unresolved authority and SHALL NOT create numbered CONTROL documents unless the current CWC explicitly authorizes a named CONTROL.

---

## 6. Authority hierarchy

The following hierarchy governs NBEF authority. A lower level SHALL NOT override a higher level. This hierarchy SHALL NOT override applicable law.

| Level | Authority |
|---|---|
| **LEVEL 1** | Explicit Human Engineer decisions recorded through controlled NBEF CWCs |
| **LEVEL 2** | Canonical NBEF CONTROL documents after Human Engineer acceptance |
| **LEVEL 3** | Explicitly adopted external/control-framework references |
| **LEVEL 4** | Human Engineer source material, historical drafts, BlueprintLiberty material, essays, speeches, podcasts, and prior discussions used as informative drafting evidence |
| **LEVEL 5** | External factual/legal/research material used for verification and constraints |
| **UNKNOWN** | Anything whose provenance or authority cannot be established |

This CONTROL, accepted through NBEF-CWC #5, is the Level 2 Root CONTROL.

LEVEL 3 material has effect in NBEF only where a Level 1 decision or a later Human Engineer–authorized CWC expressly adopts a named reference. This CONTROL adopts no Level 3 reference.

LEVEL 4 material is informative only unless a Level 1 decision or later Human Engineer–authorized CWC expressly adopts identified content into the NBEF control-document hierarchy.

LEVEL 5 material may constrain or verify later work. It SHALL NOT automatically define NBEF architecture. This CONTROL does not embed a legal survey.

UNKNOWN material SHALL NOT be used as NBEF CONTROL authority.

Discovery of a source does not make that source authoritative. NBEF-CWC #2 is a discovery record. Appearance in that inventory does not confer Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3 authority.

---

## 7. Subordinate CONTROLs

Later numbered CONTROLs derive from this CONTROL and SHALL NOT override it.

A later CONTROL SHALL NOT restate-as-new Root authority. It MAY cite this CONTROL.

Numbered CONTROL documents exist only when the Human Engineer authorizes them through a CWC.

Each adopted NBEF rule SHALL have one authoritative CONTROL home.

If a proposed sentence has no authorized home, it SHALL be omitted. A home SHALL NOT be invented.

Detailed education doctrine, operating rules, implementation concepts, and later interfaces belong in later CONTROLs if and when separately authorized. They are not created by this CONTROL.

---

## 8. Terminology governance

NBEF terms are defined only in their authoritative NBEF home.

This CONTROL defines Root identity and Root-lock terms required by existing Human Engineer decisions. It does not mint additional defined Root architecture terms.

NBBF terminology SHALL NOT automatically acquire the same meaning inside NBEF.

Any later NBBF dependency or reference SHALL identify its authoritative source and intended NBEF relationship.

The following are **not** defined CONTROL 00 architecture terms:

- **Community Schools** is a candidate NBEF implementation concept. It is reserved to later CONTROLs.
- **Organic Community Schools** is historical/predecessor terminology requiring later reconciliation. It is not defined here.

Neither term is elevated by this CONTROL. This CONTROL does not reconcile them.

---

## 9. Fail-closed unresolved authority

If authority, source status, scope, or adoption status is unresolved, work SHALL stop and return the question to the Human Engineer.

Silence does not authorize invention.

UNKNOWN material SHALL NOT be used to close a gap.

Do not invent architecture, doctrine, formulas, rights frameworks, governance rules, or CONTROL text to close a gap.

A provision that cannot be traced under Section 11 SHALL be omitted.

---

## 10. Prohibition against unauthorized invention

The following are prohibited:

- inventing NBEF architecture or education doctrine;
- silently creating CONTROL authority;
- copying the NBBF CONTROL tree into this repository;
- silently importing NBBF doctrine;
- treating NBBF CONTROLs as NBEF CONTROLs;
- making NBEF subordinate in a way that destroys separate repository authority;
- treating historical drafts, essays, conversations, podcast material, or advocacy writing as canonical NBEF authority without Human Engineer review;
- assigning NBBF meanings to NBEF terms by default;
- treating BlueprintLiberty publication as controlling NBEF authority;
- implementing or importing Runtime Republic, CDT, UNBKE, Digital Twin, API, or software architecture as NBEF doctrine;
- declaring current Kansas or federal law to be the desired NBEF architecture;
- filling gaps with invented doctrine;
- auto-creating later CONTROLs.

Every NBEF CONTROL provision SHALL be traceable to:

## 12. Amendment and replacement

NBEF CONTROL documents, including this CONTROL, may be created, adopted, amended, or withdrawn only by the Human Engineer through a controlled CWC.

These Root locks remain subject to later Human Engineer amendment through a controlled CWC.

A later CWC SHALL NOT auto-start from this CONTROL.

---

## 13. Anti-drift

The following anti-drift safeguards are Root locks:

- one-authoritative-home discipline;
- no silent import of another framework;
- no treating essays, conversations, podcasts, advocacy, or similar sources as canonical without Human Engineer review;
- no converting discovered or candidate material into normative CONTROL text merely because it was discovered or appears useful;
- no resolving UNKNOWN items by inference;
- no cross-repository modification in the name of NBEF completeness.

---

## 14. CURRENT STATE, NBEF PROPOSED STATE, and TRANSITION

Later NBEF work SHALL distinguish:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CURRENT STATE** | What law and existing institutions presently require |
| **NBEF PROPOSED STATE** | What NBEF proposes should exist |
| **TRANSITION** | Where necessary: constitutional, statutory, regulatory, institutional, or operational changes required to move between CURRENT STATE and NBEF PROPOSED STATE |

Kansas and federal education law SHALL be treated as factual/legal constraints and baseline authority where applicable.

Existing law SHALL NOT automatically define the desired NBEF architecture.

This CONTROL does not embed a legal survey. Legal-factual collection remains a later CWC matter using Level 5 sources designated by the Human Engineer.

---

NBEF MAY later explicitly reference named NBBF concepts only where Human Engineer authority permits it. Any such later reference SHALL identify its authoritative source and intended NBEF relationship. This CONTROL adopts no NBBF CONTROL.
