2. **“Education” in BlueprintLiberty** means civic training for public office, not the Node-Based Education Framework.
3. **“Node” in NBBF** means a budget/authority Node. An education node is not defined. Copying NBBF ontology would invent NBEF architecture.
4. **“Signal” in NBBF** is governmental reporting/non-interference. “Education Signals” named in this CWC’s search list are not specified in any found NBEF document.
5. **“School district” in NBBF** is a governmental-jurisdiction example. NBBF states that is not NBEF.
6. **NBBF CONTROL numbering drift** between nested tree (`07` Signal, `08` Audit) and reconciliation tree (`00B` Signal, `07` Audit, `08` Recovery). NBEF must not guess numbering.
7. **“Runtime Republic” vs CDT vs UNBKE Runtime vs Digital Twin of the Republic.** Used overlappingly in Human Engineer/CWC language; NBBF Root names CDT and UNBKE, not “Runtime Republic.”
8. **“Community Schools” vs “Organic Community Schools” vs NBEF.** Only the CE-Engineer conversation used Organic Community Schools; NBEF repo does not define the term.
9. **Four-month review.** NBBF CONTROL 05 *permits possible later use* by NBEF. That is not NBEF adoption.
10. **Informative BlueprintLiberty URLs inside NBBF** are not NBBF CONTROL authority and are not NBEF authority.
