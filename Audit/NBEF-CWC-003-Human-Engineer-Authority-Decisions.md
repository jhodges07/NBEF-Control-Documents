## Decision 1 — Framework identity

**NBEF** means **Node-Based Education Framework**.

NBEF is an independent framework.

NBEF SHALL NOT be treated as:

- a subdivision of NBBF
- a renamed NBBF
- an implementation of UNBKE
- an implementation of CDT
- an implementation of Runtime Republic
- a collection of BlueprintLiberty essays
- a conventional school-reform program

---

## Decision 2 — NBBF relationship

NBBF and NBEF are separate frameworks.

NBEF MAY later explicitly reference applicable NBBF concepts where Human Engineer authority permits it.

Potential later reference subjects include:

- Nodes
- Signals
- transparency
- accountability
- measurable performance
- review
- taxpayer stewardship
- public APIs
- recovery/clawback concepts
- jurisdiction/local-control structures

Nothing from NBBF is automatically inherited by NBEF.

NBBF terminology SHALL NOT automatically acquire the same meaning inside NBEF.

Any later NBBF dependency or reference SHALL identify its authoritative source and intended NBEF relationship.

## Decision 3 — Community Schools

**Community Schools** is accepted as a **candidate NBEF implementation concept**.

**Organic Community Schools** SHALL presently be classified as **historical/predecessor terminology** requiring later reconciliation.

Neither term is automatically a defined CONTROL 00 architecture term. Elevation into CONTROL 00 defined terms is forbidden unless required by the approved CONTROL 00 drafting charter. The charter does not require that elevation.

## Decision 5 — Runtime Republic / CDT / UNBKE

For NBEF architectural purposes:

- **Runtime Republic** represents a broader governmental operating concept within the Human Engineer’s overall work.
- **NBEF** governs the education framework/domain.
- **CDT**, **UNBKE**, software implementations, Digital Twins, and Runtime Republic concepts SHALL NOT silently become NBEF doctrine.

Future NBEF controls MAY define explicit interfaces or relationships where separately authorized.

This CWC does not implement those relationships.

## Decision 6 — CONTROL 00 purpose

CONTROL 00 SHALL be the future **NBEF Root Control**.

Its intended purpose is to establish the constitutional/architectural root of NBEF, including appropriate subjects such as:

- framework identity
- purpose
- scope
- root authority
- authority hierarchy
- subordinate-control relationship
- independence from external frameworks
- terminology governance
- fail-closed behavior
- prohibition against unauthorized invention
- provenance
- amendment/replacement governance
- anti-drift safeguards
- boundaries of subordinate controls

CONTROL 00 SHALL NOT attempt to define the complete education architecture.

Detailed doctrine SHALL belong in later controls under one-authoritative-home discipline.

This CWC authorizes a CONTROL 00 **drafting charter**. It does **not** authorize creation of CONTROL 00 itself.

## Decision 7 — Current law

Kansas and federal education law SHALL be treated as factual/legal constraints and baseline authority where applicable.

Existing law SHALL NOT automatically define the desired NBEF architecture.

Future work SHALL distinguish:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CURRENT STATE** | What law and existing institutions presently require |
| **NBEF PROPOSED STATE** | What NBEF proposes should exist |
| **TRANSITION** | Where necessary: constitutional, statutory, regulatory, institutional, or operational changes required to move between CURRENT STATE and NBEF PROPOSED STATE |

No legal research was authorized by this CWC except as required merely to classify a source already discovered. None was required.
