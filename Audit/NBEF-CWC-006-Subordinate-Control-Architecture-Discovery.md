6. **IFACE vs UNBKE/CDT/Runtime Republic repositories.** Specifying those systems inside NBEF would import them as doctrine (CONTROL 00 §10). Interface text could attempt to live in both NBEF and the other repository. Not reconciled.
7. **Control Steward vs education roles.** Control Steward is a repository-bound agent role (CWC #1). It SHALL NOT become an education organization/role CONTROL.
