A later CWC must explicitly authorize creation of the CONTROL 00 file.

---

## 1. Authorized purpose of CONTROL 00

CONTROL 00 SHALL be the **NBEF Root Control**.

Its authorized purpose is to establish the constitutional/architectural root of the Node-Based Education Framework (NBEF), limited to root-lock subjects listed in Section 2.

CONTROL 00 exists to:

- identify NBEF;
- state that NBEF is an independent framework;
- establish root authority and the Human Engineer as the authority that creates, adopts, amends, or withdraws NBEF CONTROLs;
- bind subordinate CONTROLs;
- govern terminology, provenance, amendment, fail-closed behavior, and anti-drift;
- reserve detailed education architecture to later CONTROLs under one-authoritative-home discipline.

CONTROL 00 SHALL NOT attempt to define the complete education architecture.

---

## 2. Authorized subject boundaries

A future CONTROL 00 draft MAY address **only** the following subjects, and only to the extent already decided at Level 1 or restatable without inventing education doctrine:

1. Framework identity: NBEF means Node-Based Education Framework.
2. Independence: NBEF is not a subdivision of NBBF, a renamed NBBF, an implementation of UNBKE, CDT, or Runtime Republic, a collection of BlueprintLiberty essays, or a conventional school-reform program.
3. Purpose of the Root Control (this charter’s Section 1), not a complete education-program purpose.
4. Scope of the Root Control versus later CONTROLs.
5. Root authority and Human Engineer authority.
6. Authority hierarchy consistent with the accepted source hierarchy (charter Section 5), including that the hierarchy SHALL NOT override applicable law.
7. Subordinate-control relationship: later numbered CONTROLs derive from CONTROL 00 and may not override it.
8. Independence from external frameworks (NBBF, BlueprintLiberty, Runtime Republic, CDT, UNBKE, and other non-NBEF sources).
9. Terminology governance: NBEF terms are defined only in their authoritative NBEF home; NBBF terms do not automatically mean the same thing in NBEF.
10. Fail-closed behavior for unresolved authority, unknown provenance, and missing doctrine.
11. Prohibition against unauthorized invention of architecture or CONTROL authority.
12. Provenance requirements (charter Section 6).
13. Amendment and replacement governance of CONTROLs through Human Engineer–authorized CWCs.
14. Anti-drift safeguards (including one-authoritative-home; no silent import; no treating essays/conversations as canonical).
15. Boundaries of subordinate controls: detailed doctrine belongs in later CONTROLs.
16. Distinction among CURRENT STATE, NBEF PROPOSED STATE, and TRANSITION as a governance rule for later work (charter Section 9). No legal corpus is to be drafted into CONTROL 00 by this charter.
17. Reservation that later NBEF CONTROLs MAY, if separately authorized, define explicit interfaces to Runtime Republic, CDT, UNBKE, or software/Digital Twin systems — without implementing or specifying those interfaces in CONTROL 00.
18. Reservation that NBEF MAY later explicitly reference named NBBF concepts only with identified source and intended NBEF relationship — without adopting any NBBF CONTROL in CONTROL 00.

## 3. Prohibited subject expansion

A CONTROL 00 draft SHALL NOT:

- define the complete education architecture;
- define curriculum, pedagogy, testing batteries, or performance formulas;
- define parent-rights, student-rights, or teacher doctrine beyond stating that such doctrine is out of Root scope;
- define school-governance, school-choice, procurement, or funding doctrine;
- define education-node ontology or education-Signal operating rules;
- define Community Schools or Organic Community Schools as CONTROL 00 architecture terms;
- copy the NBBF CONTROL tree or silently inherit NBBF doctrine;
- assign NBBF meanings to NBEF terms by default;
- treat BlueprintLiberty publication as controlling NBEF authority;
- implement or import Runtime Republic, CDT, UNBKE, Digital Twin, API, or software architecture;
- declare current Kansas or federal law to be the desired NBEF architecture;
- perform or embed new legal research;
- create APIs, code, or operational school systems;
- auto-create later CONTROLs;
- fill gaps with invented doctrine.

**Community Schools** remains a candidate implementation concept reserved to later CONTROLs.  
**Organic Community Schools** remains historical/predecessor terminology requiring later reconciliation.  
This charter does **not** require either term to appear as a defined CONTROL 00 architecture term. CONTROL 00 MAY omit both, or MAY record only that implementation concepts are reserved to later CONTROLs.

## 4. One-authoritative-home rule

Each adopted NBEF rule SHALL have one authoritative CONTROL home.

CONTROL 00 is the home of Root identity, Root authority, hierarchy, independence, terminology governance at Root level, fail-closed Root rules, provenance/amendment Root rules, and subordinate-control boundaries.

CONTROL 00 SHALL NOT become the home of detailed education doctrine that belongs in a later CONTROL.

A later CONTROL SHALL NOT restated-as-new Root authority. It MAY cite CONTROL 00.

If a proposed sentence has no authorized home, omit it. Do not invent a home.

## 6. Provenance requirements

Every CONTROL 00 provision SHALL be traceable to:

- a recorded Level 1 Human Engineer decision (NBEF-CWC #1 repository locks, NBEF-CWC #3 decisions, or a later CWC that amends them); or
- a fail-closed reservation expressly required by this charter (for example: detailed doctrine reserved; NBBF not inherited; unknown sources unused).

Each provision SHOULD identify:

- the NBEF home (CONTROL 00);
- that it is Root-lock text, not later-control doctrine;
- if it mentions an external framework, that the mention is independence, reservation, or a non-adopted possible later reference — not inheritance.

No provision may rely on unlocated unpublished notes, conversation memory, or persuasive completeness of NBBF or BlueprintLiberty materials.

## 7. Terminology discipline

- **NBEF** means Node-Based Education Framework only.
- NBEF is independent. It is not NBBF.
- NBBF terms (including Node, Signal, review, transparency, accountability, performance, API, recovery/clawback, jurisdiction) SHALL NOT automatically acquire NBEF meaning.
- Later NBBF references, if any, SHALL name the authoritative NBBF source and the intended NBEF relationship.
- **Community Schools** is a candidate implementation concept, not a CONTROL 00 defined architecture term under this charter.
- **Organic Community Schools** is historical/predecessor terminology, not a CONTROL 00 defined architecture term.
- **Runtime Republic** is a broader governmental operating concept in the Human Engineer’s overall work. It is not NBEF.
- **CDT** and **UNBKE** are not NBEF and SHALL NOT silently become NBEF doctrine.
- **BlueprintLiberty** material is informative unless later explicitly adopted.
- Canonical NBEF CONTROLs SHALL be Markdown (`.md`), per NBEF-CWC #1.

Do not mint additional defined Root terms unless a later Level 1 decision authorizes them.

## 8. Fail-closed requirements

If, while drafting CONTROL 00, any of the following is true, **stop that provision** and omit it or return it to the Human Engineer:

- authority, source status, or adoption status is unresolved;
- a sentence would invent education architecture, rights frameworks, formulas, or operating rules;
- a sentence would copy or silently inherit NBBF, BlueprintLiberty, CDT, UNBKE, or Runtime Republic doctrine;
- a term would be defined by analogy to NBBF without Level 1 authorization;
- provenance cannot be shown under Section 6;
- the sentence belongs in a later CONTROL under one-authoritative-home;
- the sentence requires legal research not authorized for that CWC.

Do not close a gap by invention.

## 9. CURRENT STATE / PROPOSED STATE / TRANSITION

CONTROL 00 MAY require later NBEF work to distinguish:

- **CURRENT STATE** — what law and existing institutions presently require;
- **NBEF PROPOSED STATE** — what NBEF proposes should exist;
- **TRANSITION** — where necessary, what constitutional, statutory, regulatory, institutional, or operational changes would be required to move between them.

Kansas and federal education law SHALL be treated as factual/legal constraints and baseline authority where applicable.

Existing law SHALL NOT automatically define the desired NBEF architecture.

CONTROL 00 SHALL NOT embed a legal survey. That work requires a later CWC and Level 5 sources designated by the Human Engineer.

## 10. Unresolved questions that would block CONTROL 00 drafting

CONTROL 00 file creation is **blocked** until all of the following are true:

1. **File-creation authorization.** A CWC after NBEF-CWC #3 explicitly authorizes creation of the CONTROL 00 file. This charter does not.
2. **Path and filename.** The Human Engineer or that later CWC designates the exact path (this CWC forbids creating `controls/00_Control.md` here; a later CWC must name the authorized path).
3. **In-bounds content only.** The draft is limited to Sections 2–3 of this charter. Any required-looking subject that cannot be written from Level 1 without invention SHALL be a reservation, not invented text.
4. **No NBBF adoption packet is required for Root.** CONTROL 00 must not wait for, and must not include, adopted NBBF CONTROL citations. Later named references are a later CWC matter.
5. **No legal-research packet is required for Root.** CONTROL 00 must not wait for Kansas/federal research. It may only install the distinction in Section 9.
6. **No unpublished-notes packet is required for Root.** Unknown notes remain UNKNOWN and unused. Their absence does not authorize invention and does not expand Root scope.
7. **Community Schools / Organic Community Schools** are not to be defined in CONTROL 00 under this charter. A draft that defines them as Root architecture is out of charter and SHALL NOT be committed as CONTROL 00.
8. **No GitHub publication** is required to draft CONTROL 00. Local Git remains sufficient until separately authorized.

If a later CWC authorizes CONTROL 00 but the draft exceeds this charter, fail closed: do not create the CONTROL; return the excess to the Human Engineer.
